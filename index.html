Circular Design Advisor

Reuse, repair, reprocessing, recycling or disposal of a modular aluminium exhibition-booth panel: per-use LCA, circularity KPIs, scenarios and uncertainty.

Interactive dashboard →Change the design, business model, uses and distances; KPIs, scenario lab, Monte Carlo, optimiser and inventory recalculate in your browser. LCI workbook viewer →Every sheet of the Excel life cycle inventory, with the formula behind each calculated cell. Download Excel workbook ↓Formula-driven inventory, GWP by stage, cost and KPIs, with a validation sheet against the Python model. Animated report →Static animated export of the Streamlit dashboard charts (large file).

Illustrative data: only 6 of 26 inputs come from literature or public databases; the rest are flagged assumptions. Source code, methods and tests: github.com/malleshkomal/circular-design-advisor.
